Signal and Noise Simulator

Everything here is simulated and invented for practice. Nothing is saved or sent. Each button draws fresh random samples, so your numbers will differ a little from the numbers in the feedback, and the feedback gives ranges for that reason.

Free play

Group 1 has a true mean of 100. Group 2 has a true mean of 100 minus the true difference. Set the controls, then draw one study or run 1,000 studies.

Five guided experiments

Each experiment asks you to commit to a prediction first. Then the simulator sets the controls, runs the studies, and compares what happened with what you expected.

Return to the reading with Mark as done on this page when you have tried all five.
