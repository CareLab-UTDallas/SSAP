Budget: 20 min · Complete every part

Here, nothing is real. Every number below is random noise, so there is no true difference anywhere. A test at α = .05 should still flag about 5 percent of such comparisons. This lab shows what happens when a researcher keeps looking until something works. Everything is simulated for practice.

Part 1. You are the researcher

A study compares two groups of 20 people on up to 20 different outcome measures. The groups do not differ on any of them. Test the outcomes one at a time, and stop as soon as you find a result you could publish.

Part 2. How often does hunting find something?

Choose how many outcomes a researcher tests per study. The lab runs 1,000 studies with no true effect and counts how many contain at least one p below .05.

Part 3. What pre-registration does

Pre-registration means writing down your question, your variables, your test, and your cutoff before you see the results. You cannot go hunting afterward, because the plan is already on record. You will pre-register your own project plan in the Research Project module.
